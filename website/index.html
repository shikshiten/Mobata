ARCHITECTURAL ADVANTAGES
[TCP] NETWORK SHIELD

Anti-Throttling Protocol

Employs ConnectionTcpObfuscated to prevent restrictive Wi-Fi routers and ISP deep-packet inspection (DPI) from resetting connection handshakes.

Card 1 of 6
[STREAM] PLAYBACK

In-App Video Streaming

Flags every uploaded video with supports_streaming=True so videos play immediately inside Telegram channels without waiting for full download.

Card 2 of 6
[AUTO] RATE RESILIENCE

Self-Healing FloodWait

Automatically intercepts Telegram rate-limit challenges, sleeps for the exact server-requested seconds, and resumes without crashing or losing position.

Card 3 of 6
[LOCK] CONCURRENCY

Instance Protection

Integrated atomic process locking stops overlapping instances, completely eliminating the "Server replied with a wrong session ID" error.

Card 4 of 6
[QUEUE] SCHEDULER

Smart Priority Sorting

Prioritizes small photos first so thousands of files upload rapidly, ensuring continuous progress before tackling massive 4K video files.

Card 5 of 6
[SYNC] PERSISTENCE

Zero-Loss Persistence

Records uploaded paths to uploaded_history.txt after every success. Power cuts or laptop restarts won't re-upload duplicates.
